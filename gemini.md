# Development Roadmap: Feature Gaps & Enhancements

## Phase 1: Core Automation (Completed ✅)
1. **Notification: Client Photo Selection**: In-App & Real Email notifications.
2. **Full Folder Upload**: Recursive folder upload to Google Drive.
3. **Two-Way Calendar Sync**: Real-time sync with deletion support.
4. **Automatic Watermarking**: Frontend overlay for unpaid projects.
5. **Large Project Download**: Batching ZIP parts.
6. **Advanced Drive Management**: CRUD operations within the app.

---

## Phase 2: Professional Excellence (Completed ✅)
7. **Smart Testimonials (Review Filter)**: Protect Google Business rating.
8. **Proofing Comments & Annotations**: Specific instructions per photo.
9. **Financial Data Export (CSV)**: Export reports for accounting.
10. **Automated Reminders (Cron Jobs)**: Daily H-1 session reminders.
11. **Conversion & Business Analytics**: Lead conversion funnel tracking.

---

## Phase 3: Scaling & Enterprise (Completed ✅)
12. **RBAC (Role Based Access Control)**: Staff vs Owner dashboard permissions.
13. **Secure Watermarking (Sharp)**: Backend-level image processing for high security.
14. **Emergency Email Notification**: Instant alerts for negative client feedback.
15. **Monthly Financial Aggregator**: Automatic monthly metrics calculation.
16. **SMTP Ready Structure**: Implemented using Nodemailer with Ethereal/Production placeholders.

---

## Future Roadmap (Scaling Up)
* **Async ZIP Generation**: Transitioning to Cloud Tasks for massive (10k+) galleries.
* **WhatsApp Business API**: Direct automated messaging without manual confirmation.
* **Mobile Native Apps**: Building Flutter/Kotlin apps for on-the-go management.
